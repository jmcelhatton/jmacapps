Join a beta

Most JMacApps apps are tested in public betas through Apple’s TestFlight app before they reach the App Store. When a beta is open, its app page has a “Join the TestFlight beta” button. For a beta that hasn’t opened yet, email us with the app name and we’ll send you an invite as soon as it’s ready.

Common questions

How do I install a TestFlight beta?

Install TestFlight from the App Store, then tap the beta link on the app’s page here. TestFlight installs the app and keeps it up to date with each new build.

Do I have to use voice input?

No. Voice is there to save time, but every question can also be answered by typing.

How do I report a bug in a beta?

Take a screenshot inside the beta app and TestFlight will offer to send it as feedback, or email us with a description of what happened.

Is Meal Memories the same app as MealFavs Pro?

Yes. Meal Memories is the new name and new version of MealFavs Pro. [Confirm: your saved meals carry over when you update]
