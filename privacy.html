Privacy policy

Your information stays yours.

Draft for review. Each highlighted item must be confirmed or corrected before this page goes live, because the App Store links to it for every app.

Effective [date]. This policy covers all apps published by JMacApps: Meal Memories (MealFavs Pro), Photo Filing Cabinet AI, Jim Handy, Golf Journal, Life Events Tracker and PDF Knowledge Library.

The short version

  • JMacApps does not sell your information or show ads. [confirm]
  • JMacApps apps have no sign-up and no JMacApps account. [confirm]
  • Your entries are stored on your device. Apps that offer iCloud sync also keep a copy in your own private iCloud account.

Information you enter

Meals, documents, jobs, clients, rounds, events and other entries you create are saved on your device using Apple’s storage frameworks. When iCloud sync is available and turned on, your entries are also stored in your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. JMacApps cannot see or access your iCloud data.

Voice input

When an app asks you questions out loud, it uses Apple’s built-in voice, which runs entirely on your device. When you answer by voice, Apple’s speech recognition turns what you say into text. This mostly happens on your device, but Apple may process some audio on its own servers to improve accuracy. That processing is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. JMacApps never receives or stores your voice recordings. iOS asks your permission before any app can use the microphone or speech recognition.

Contacts, invoices and sharing

Jim Handy can read a contact you pick from your Contacts to fill in a client’s details; it reads only the contact you choose. Invoices and estimates are created on your device and leave it only when you send them yourself from Mail or the share sheet.

Location

Life Events Tracker, Golf Journal, Photo Filing Cabinet AI, Jim Handy and PDF Knowledge Library do not collect your location. In Jim Handy, an address is text you type or copy from a contact you choose. In Golf Journal, a course is simply a name you type or save. In Photo Filing Cabinet AI, if a photo from your library already contains location information, that information stays inside the photo on your device; the app does not add or share it.

Meal Memories asks for your location only while you are using it, and only if you allow it. It uses your location to save where you ate a meal (the place name, address and map position), to show meals on a map, and to find restaurants near you. That information is saved with your meals on your device and in your private iCloud account. Apple’s Maps service receives your position to look up addresses and nearby restaurants. When you use Restaurant Find with AI turned on, the area you searched and the nearby restaurant names and addresses are sent to Google Gemini, but your exact GPS position is not. A meal’s location leaves the app otherwise only when you choose to share that meal or open it in another app.

AI features

The Event Assistant in Life Events Tracker and the Round Assistant in Golf Journal and the voice assistant in Jim Handy use only Apple’s built-in tools. They do not send your events, rounds, jobs, clients or notes to any AI company.

Meal Memories uses Apple’s on-device AI first when your iPhone supports it. If that isn’t available, or you choose Cloud in Settings, it uses Google Gemini. When you tap Analyze with AI, the meal photo (reduced in size), plus the meal name and serving count if you entered them, is sent to Google Gemini to describe the meal and estimate calories. When you use Restaurant Find with AI turned on, the area you searched, your chosen cuisine, and the nearby restaurant names, addresses, distances and your own ratings are sent to Google Gemini to rank the results. Nothing is sent until you use one of these features, and the app asks your permission first. You can choose On-device in Settings to keep meal photos from being sent to Google. Google’s handling of this information is covered by Google’s Gemini API terms.

Photo Filing Cabinet AI reads the text on scanned documents and PDFs on your device, using Apple’s built-in tools. When you analyze a scanned document or PDF with AI, and only after you agree, up to the first 3,000 characters of that text, plus your folder names, are sent to Google Gemini to suggest a title, summary and folder. The document image and PDF file are not sent. Because this is the text printed on your document, it can include personal details, so you can turn AI analysis off in Settings at any time. Google’s handling is covered by the Gemini API terms.

PDF Knowledge Library creates document summaries with Apple’s on-device AI. The text of your PDF is read and summarized on your Mac and is not sent to any AI company. Your PDFs, tags, notes and summaries stay on your Mac.

Analytics and crash reports

Meal Memories, Life Events Tracker, Golf Journal, Photo Filing Cabinet AI, Jim Handy and PDF Knowledge Library contain no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and nothing in them tracks you across other apps or websites. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports.

This website

If you send a message through the suggestion form, what you write, and your email address if you include it, is delivered to us by [form service, e.g. Formspree]. We use it only to read your suggestion and, if you asked, to reply. This website has no ads and no tracking.

Children

JMacApps apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
