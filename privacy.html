The short version

  • JMacApps does not sell your information or show ads. [confirm]
  • JMacApps apps have no sign-up and no JMacApps account. [confirm]
  • Your entries are stored on your device. Apps that offer iCloud sync also keep a copy in your own private iCloud account.

Information you enter

Meals, documents, jobs, clients, rounds, events and other entries you create are saved on your device using Apple’s storage frameworks. When iCloud sync is available and turned on, your entries are also stored in your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. JMacApps cannot see or access your iCloud data.

Voice input

When an app asks you questions out loud, it uses Apple’s built-in voice, which runs entirely on your device. When you answer by voice, Apple’s speech recognition turns what you say into text. This mostly happens on your device, but Apple may process some audio on its own servers to improve accuracy. That processing is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. JMacApps never receives or stores your voice recordings. iOS asks your permission before any app can use the microphone or speech recognition.

Location

Life Events Tracker does not use your location at all.

Meal Memories asks for your location only while you are using it, and only if you allow it. It uses your location to save where you ate a meal (the place name, address and map position), to show meals on a map, and to find restaurants near you. That information is saved with your meals on your device and in your private iCloud account. Apple’s Maps service receives your position to look up addresses and nearby restaurants. When you use Restaurant Find with AI turned on, the area you searched and the nearby restaurant names and addresses are sent to Google Gemini, but your exact GPS position is not. A meal’s location leaves the app otherwise only when you choose to share that meal or open it in another app.

AI features

The Event Assistant in Life Events Tracker uses only Apple’s built-in tools on your device. It does not send your events to any AI company.

Meal Memories uses Apple’s on-device AI first when your iPhone supports it. If that isn’t available, or you choose Cloud in Settings, it uses Google Gemini. When you tap Analyze with AI, the meal photo (reduced in size), plus the meal name and serving count if you entered them, is sent to Google Gemini to describe the meal and estimate calories. When you use Restaurant Find with AI turned on, the area you searched, your chosen cuisine, and the nearby restaurant names, addresses, distances and your own ratings are sent to Google Gemini to rank the results. Nothing is sent until you use one of these features, and the app asks your permission first. You can choose On-device in Settings to keep meal photos from being sent to Google. Google’s handling of this information is covered by Google’s Gemini API terms.

Meal Memories and Life Events Tracker contain no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and nothing in them tracks you across other apps or websites.
