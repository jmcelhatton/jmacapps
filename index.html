How every app works

Stop typing.
Start talking.

Each app walks you through adding something with a few short spoken questions. Prefer the keyboard? You can type instead at any step.

  1. 1
    Tap the micStart from the microphone button at the top of any list.
  2. 2
    Answer out loudThe app asks one simple question at a time, like “What is the job?”
  3. 3
    Review and saveCheck what the app filled in, change anything you like, and save.
