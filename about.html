About

A small studio with big ideas.

JMacApps makes practical apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Each one starts with an everyday problem, removes the busywork, and lets you add things by simply saying them.

Who we are

John Barry McElhatton

Founder · iOS developer since 2018

John is a retired FBI Special Agent. Toward the end of his 26-year career he specialized in computer forensics, working closely with U.S. Attorneys and FBI case agents to locate, retrieve and analyze evidence of federal crime on subject computers and networks.

In retirement he founded 3D Forensics Inc., working with law firms, corporations and individuals to investigate, report and testify on complex computer forensic cases. Since 2018 John has concentrated on creating iOS apps for iPhone and iPad.

“Captain” Dan Bonelli

Co-founder · iOS developer since 2024

Dan is an electrical engineer who worked for IBM for 20 years, holding multiple executive positions in Application, Middleware and Client Services. Since 2024 he has concentrated on developing apps for iPhone and iPad.

How we build

  • Native apps written in SwiftUI and SwiftData, made to feel at home on Apple devices.
  • Voice first: every app can be used by talking, with the keyboard always available.
  • Small and focused: each app does one job well.
  • Built in the open with testers: every app goes through a public TestFlight beta before release.
