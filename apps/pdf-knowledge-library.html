PDF Knowledge Library

For Mac

Every PDF you own, tagged, searchable and summarized.

Keep manuals, recipes, travel plans, checklists and every other PDF in one Mac library. Scan a folder to bring in new files, organize them with simple tags, find any document by title, filename or tag, even by voice, and let AI summarize it and read the summary aloud.

What you can do

Organize with simple tags

Group PDFs by topic so manuals, travel, recipes, golf and everything else stay easy to find. Mark the ones you use most as favorites.

Search by typing or talking

Find any document by title, filename or tag, or tap the mic and just say what you’re looking for.

AI summaries, read aloud

Get a short summary of any PDF and have it read aloud. Say yes to save it or no to delete it.

Build your library quickly

Scan a folder to add new PDFs. Files already in your library are skipped automatically.

Preview without leaving

Read each PDF right in the app, or reveal it in Finder and open it in Preview with a right-click.
