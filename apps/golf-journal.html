Golf Journal

For iPhone and iPad

Your golf game, in one simple journal.

Keep your rounds, scores, handicap estimate, courses, practice sessions and drills together in one simple journal. The Round Assistant logs a round by voice, one question at a time, and your handicap estimate updates as you play.

What you can do

Round Assistant

Log a round by voice, one step at a time: date, course, score and more. Tap Repeat to hear a question again, or type instead.

Handicap estimate

See an estimate based on the average of your best differentials from your most recent rounds.

Your courses, saved

Save each course with its tees, course rating and slope. Saved courses become quick picks when you log a round, by tap or by voice.

Every round at a glance

Score, course, tees, date and who you played with, all on one list.

Practice with purpose

Write down what you’re working on before practice, then note how it went afterward.

Drills you’ll actually use

Build drills of up to six numbered steps, add an optional YouTube link, and put them in the order you like.
